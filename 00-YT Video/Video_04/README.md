## 04 - PyTorch Training Pipeline

This section covers the PyTorch Training Pipeline based on the following tutorial video.

## 📹 Video Source

- **YouTube Tutorial:** [PyTorch Training Pipeline | Video 4 | CampusX](https://youtu.be/MKxEbbKpL5Q)

## 📌 Overview

This tutorial covers how to build a complete training pipeline in 
PyTorch — combining tensors, autograd, and gradient descent into a 
structured workflow. It walks through the core training loop: making 
predictions, computing loss, calculating gradients with `.backward()`, 
and updating model parameters step by step.
